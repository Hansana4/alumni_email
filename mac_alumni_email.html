MAC alumni!!

You guys have been at 200+ companies, and it's likely that other alumni are working at the same company as you! If you want to see for yourself, head over to our MAC Alumni Network. We have mapped out every current and past company (excluding standard part-time jobs) of our former MAC members into a network graph.

With such an amazing spread of talented people, we think it's time we started building out MAC's alumni program, with these three goals in mind:

 
To provide a sense of community, belonging and balance that many graduates may lose.
 
To provide MAC alumni with the connections, opportunities and resources to accelerate their careers.
 
To build the strongest IT postgraduate network in Australia.

We want to produce something that you would be proud to be associated with. We want events that alumni genuinely want to attend.

To kick things off, we're doing something fun:
